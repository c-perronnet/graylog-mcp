| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-03-05-02 | Tampering | confirmationToken hash drifts due to a future change in computeCascadeHash canonicalization → snapshot 5/6 fixtures break silently in CI | mitigate | The 64-hex hash IS the fixture content — any canonicalization drift causes a loud snapshot mismatch and CI red.
